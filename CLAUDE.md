# geochecker — DORMANT (the published package, not the product)

> **DORMANT.** Last commit 2026-07-10.

This is the standalone open-source GEO scorer published to **public npm** as
`@dariodario/geochecker` — dario-1 consumes it for GEO types and the version/stars
display only. **The live scan engine is elsewhere**: `dd-1/dd/geo-api`, behind
dario-1's `PUBLIC_GEO_API_BASE`. Looking for how a scan actually runs? It is there,
not here.
